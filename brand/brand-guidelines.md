## Brand Identity

### Mission Statement
Lumorius empowers enterprise teams with intelligent automation — turning meetings, documents, and goals into coordinated action. Where knowledge becomes light.

### Brand Personality
**Luminous.** **Precise.** **Trustworthy.** **Visionary.**

Lumorius should feel like clarity arriving: the moment complexity resolves into insight. Enterprise-grade without being cold. Intelligent without being intimidating.

### Name
**Lumorius** — from *lumen* (Latin: light, illumination) + *glorious*. The brand communicates intelligence as illumination: focused insight cutting through complexity.

## The Lumorius Mark

### Concept
A **radiant focal point** — a thin outer circle containing a diamond (rhombus) at center, with eight precisely tapered rays (four cardinal, four diagonal) reaching outward. The diamond represents the lens: focused vision, decision intelligence, geometric precision. The rays represent knowledge radiating outward: illumination, reach, connected intelligence. The outer ring unifies and contains.

### Symbol Anatomy
- **Outer ring:** thin circle, 30% opacity — the boundary of the enterprise ecosystem
- **Cardinal rays (N, S, E, W):** full-opacity tapered polygons — primary knowledge directions
- **Diagonal rays (NE, SE, SW, NW):** 45–50% opacity tapered polygons — secondary connective intelligence
- **Diamond outline:** large rhombus, ~12% fill — the lens frame, vision
- **Diamond core:** filled inner rhombus — the focal point, concentrated intelligence
- **Center dot:** bright, near-white — the precise moment of insight

## Color System

### Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Background Dark | Midnight | `#050C1A` | App background, icon background |
| Background Deep | Abyss | `#0A1628` | Secondary surfaces |
| Gold Bright | Luminance | `#FCD34D` | Ray tips, highlights |
| Gold Core | Amber | `#F59E0B` | Primary brand accent, CTAs |
| Gold Deep | Burnished | `#D97706` | Ray roots, pressed states |
| Gold Darkest | Ember | `#B45309` | Light-mode primary mark |
| Near-white | Ivory | `#FEF3C7` | Center dot, high-contrast text on dark |

### Monochrome
The mark works in pure black or pure white using `currentColor` (see `logo-monochrome.svg`).

### Do Not
- Use the retired indigo/violet/cyan palette (`#6366F1`, `#8B5CF6`, `#06B6D4`) — those colors are retired
- Use gray or silver as the primary brand accent
- Apply gradients to the wordmark text

## Typography

### Primary: Inter (Google Fonts)
Used for UI, wordmark, and all digital surfaces.

```
font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif;
```

### Wordmark Specifications
- Font: Inter 700 (Bold)
- Letter-spacing: −0.5px
- Case: Title case (`Lumorius`)
- Never all-caps in the wordmark

### Tagline
- Font: Inter 400 (Regular)
- Letter-spacing: +3.5px
- Case: ALL CAPS (`ENTERPRISE AI PLATFORM`)
- Color: `#64748B` (Slate 500)

## Brand Voice

**Precise:** Say exactly what you mean. No filler words.  
**Confident:** State things directly. Avoid hedging language.  
**Clear:** Write for clarity, not cleverness.  
**Human:** Enterprise software, but written for people.
