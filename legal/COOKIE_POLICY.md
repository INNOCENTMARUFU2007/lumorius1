# Cookie Policy — Lumorius AI Platform

**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026

## What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files stored on your device to enable Service functionality and analytics.

## Cookies We Use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| `lumorius_session` | Essential | Maintain authentication session | Session |
| `lumorius_csrf` | Essential | CSRF protection | Session |
| `lumorius_refresh` | Essential | Refresh token (HTTP-only, Secure) | 30 days |
| `lumorius_prefs` | Functional | UI preferences (theme, language) | 1 year |

## What We Don't Use
We do NOT use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

## Managing Cookies
You can clear cookies via your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies will prevent login.
