# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
**Between:** INMARTECH PVT LTD ("Processor")  
**And:** Customer ("Controller")  
**Effective Date:** Upon execution of the Master Service Agreement  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person as defined under GDPR Article 4(1) and equivalent applicable data protection laws.

1.2 **"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, erasure, or destruction.

1.3 **"Data Subject"** means the individual whose Personal Data is being processed.

1.4 **"Sub-processor"** means any third-party processor engaged by INMARTECH PVT LTD to assist in Processing Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

1.5 **"Applicable Data Protection Law"** means GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, PDPA, and any other applicable national or regional data protection legislation.

---

## 2. Scope and Purpose

2.1 This DPA applies to all Processing of Personal Data by INMARTECH PVT LTD on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Lumorius AI platform services.

2.2 INMARTECH PVT LTD shall Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required by applicable law.

2.3 The subject matter, nature, purpose, and duration of Processing, and the types of Personal Data and categories of Data Subjects, are set out in **Annex 1**.

---

## 3. Obligations of the Processor

INMARTECH PVT LTD shall:

3.1 Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller.

3.2 Ensure that all personnel authorized to Process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

3.3 Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures as described in **Annex 2**.

3.4 Not engage Sub-processors without prior written authorization from the Controller (general authorization is deemed granted for Sub-processors listed in **Annex 3**).

3.5 Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject rights requests (access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection) within 72 hours of receiving notice.

3.6 Notify the Controller without undue delay (and no later than 48 hours) after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.

3.7 Assist the Controller with Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) as required.

3.8 Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of the agreement, at the Controller's election, within 30 days.

3.9 Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, including allowing for audits conducted by the Controller or a mandated auditor (with 30 days' notice, during business hours).

---

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 INMARTECH PVT LTD shall not appoint additional Sub-processors without informing the Controller with at least 14 days' notice.

4.2 The Controller may object to the appointment within 14 days. If no resolution is reached, the Controller may terminate the affected services.

4.3 INMARTECH PVT LTD shall impose the same data protection obligations on Sub-processors as set out in this DPA.

4.4 INMARTECH PVT LTD remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors.

## 5. International Data Transfers

5.1 INMARTECH PVT LTD shall not transfer Personal Data outside the EEA without ensuring an adequate level of protection through: (a) adequacy decision; (b) Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs); or (c) other approved transfer mechanism.

5.2 Where SCCs are used, the applicable module will be incorporated by reference.

---

## 6. Security Measures (Annex 2)

INMARTECH PVT LTD implements and maintains:

- **Encryption:** AES-256 at rest; TLS 1.3 in transit
- **Access Control:** Role-based access control (RBAC); MFA required for all privileged access
- **Audit Logging:** All access to Personal Data is logged with user, timestamp, and action
- **Pseudonymisation:** Applied where technically feasible
- **Backup:** Daily encrypted backups with 30-day retention
- **Vulnerability Management:** Weekly automated dependency scans; quarterly pen testing
- **Incident Response:** Documented IRP with 48-hour notification SLA
- **Employee Training:** Annual data protection training required for all staff
