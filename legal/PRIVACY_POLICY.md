# Privacy Policy — Lumorius AI Platform

**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026
**Controller:** INMARTECH PVT LTD

## 1. Data We Collect
- **Account data:** name, email, role, organization
- **Usage data:** features accessed, actions taken, timestamps
- **Content data:** documents, meeting transcripts, messages you upload
- **Technical data:** IP address, device type, browser, logs
- **Payment data:** processed by Stripe; we store only last-4 digits and billing address

## 2. Legal Basis (GDPR)
We process data under: contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligation, and (for marketing) consent.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- Providing and improving the Service
- AI processing of your uploaded content (with your instruction)
- Security monitoring and fraud prevention
- Compliance with legal obligations
- Sending transactional emails

## 4. Data Sharing
We share data only with:
- Sub-processors (AWS, OpenAI, Stripe, Postmark) under DPA
- Law enforcement when legally required
- Acquirers in a corporate transaction (with notice)

We never sell personal data.

## 5. Data Retention
- Account data: retained for the contract term + 30 days after deletion request
- Backup copies: purged within 90 days
- Audit logs: 7 years (legal obligation)

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)
- Access, rectification, deletion, portability, restriction, objection
- Submit requests to: privacy@lumorius.com
- Response within 30 days

## 7. International Transfers
Data is processed in the EU and US. US transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. Security
We implement encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), access controls, and regular penetration testing.
