# Accessibility Policy
**Organization:** INMARTECH PVT LTD  
**Product:** Lumorius AI Enterprise Platform  
**Version:** 1.0 · June 2026  
**Standard:** WCAG 2.1 Level AA

---

## 1. Commitment

INMARTECH PVT LTD is committed to ensuring that Lumorius AI is accessible to all users, including people with disabilities. We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA and to continuously improve the accessibility of our platform.

---

## 2. Standards

Lumorius AI targets compliance with:

- **WCAG 2.1 Level AA** (primary standard)
- **Section 508** of the US Rehabilitation Act (for US government customers)
- **EN 301 549** (European accessibility standard)
- **ARIA 1.1** (Accessible Rich Internet Applications)

---

## 3. Accessibility Features

### Navigation and Structure
- All pages have descriptive `<title>` elements
- Logical heading hierarchy (H1–H6) on all pages
- Skip navigation links provided
- Keyboard navigation supported throughout the application
- Focus indicators visible for all interactive elements
- Logical tab order maintained

### Visual
- Colour contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text (WCAG AA)
- Colour contrast ratio of at least 3:1 for large text and UI components
- Information is never conveyed by colour alone
- Text can be resized up to 200% without loss of functionality
- No content flashes more than 3 times per second
- Images have descriptive alternative text

### Interactive Elements
- All form fields have visible labels
- Error messages identify the field and describe the problem
- Required fields are clearly indicated
- Buttons and links have descriptive names
- Time limits (where present) are adjustable or extendable

### AI Features
- All AI-generated content is labelled with text (not colour alone)
- Screen reader announcements for AI processing states
- AI outputs are presented in semantic HTML
- Loading states use ARIA live regions

### Support for Assistive Technologies
- Compatible with NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver screen readers
- Supports OS-level high contrast mode
- Supports OS-level reduced motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Touch targets are at least 44×44 CSS pixels

## 6. Feedback and Reporting

We welcome feedback on accessibility barriers in Lumorius AI.

**Contact:**
- Email: accessibility@lumorius.com
- Subject: "Accessibility Feedback — Lumorius AI"
- Response time: Within 5 business days

When reporting an accessibility issue, please include:
- The page or feature affected
- The barrier you encountered
- The assistive technology you are using (if applicable)
- Your operating system and browser version
