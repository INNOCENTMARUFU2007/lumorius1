## 8. Non-Competition

8.1 During employment, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in any business or activity that competes with the Company's business without prior written consent.

8.2 For a period of **6 months** following termination, the Employee shall not join, establish, or provide services to a directly competing enterprise AI operating system business operating in the same market segment as Lumorius AI.

*Note: Non-compete restrictions are enforceable in India to a reasonable extent. This clause will be interpreted narrowly to protect legitimate business interests.*

## 9. Non-Solicitation

9.1 For 12 months after termination, the Employee shall not directly solicit or recruit any Company employee or contractor with whom they had direct working contact.

9.2 For 12 months after termination, the Employee shall not solicit any customer with whom they had material contact during the preceding 12 months of employment.

## 10. Termination

10.1 **Probation Period:** The first [3/6] months of employment is a probationary period. During probation, either party may terminate with 2 weeks' notice (or payment in USD).

10.2 **After Probation — Notice Period:**
- Employee to Company: 30 days
- Company to Employee: 30 days (or payment in USD)

10.3 **Summary Termination:** The Company may terminate without notice for: (a) serious misconduct; (b) breach of this Agreement; (c) misrepresentation in the hiring process; (d) criminal conviction.

10.4 **Return of Property:** On termination, the Employee shall immediately return all Company property, including equipment, access credentials, confidential documents, and data.
