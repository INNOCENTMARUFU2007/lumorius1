# Governance Manual — INMARTECH PVT LTD

**Version:** 1.0 · **Date:** June 19, 2026

## 1. Board of Directors
- Meets quarterly (minimum)
- Approves strategy, budgets, and reserved matters
- Maintains board minutes and resolutions in secure document store

## 2. Executive Team
- CEO: Accountable for all operations
- CTO: Accountable for product and technology
- CFO: Accountable for finance and compliance

## 3. Decision Rights Matrix
| Decision | Authority |
|----------|-----------|
| Hire/Fire VP+ | Board |
| Product roadmap | CTO + CEO |
| Budget variance >20% | CFO + CEO |
| Customer contracts <$100K | Sales Lead |
| Security incidents | CISO + CEO within 72h |

## 4. Risk Oversight
- Risk register reviewed monthly by executive team
- Material risks reported to Board quarterly
- See Risk Management Framework for details

## 5. Compliance Program
- Annual SOC 2 Type II audit
- GDPR DPO designated
- Annual penetration test
- See Compliance Manual

## 6. Document Retention
All corporate records retained per applicable law (minimum 7 years).
