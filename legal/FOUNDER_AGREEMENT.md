# Founder Agreement — INMARTECH PVT LTD

**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026
**Parties:** [FOUNDER NAMES] and INMARTECH PVT LTD

## 1. Equity
Founders' equity is set out in the Shareholder Agreement. All founder shares are subject to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff.

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities
Each founder's role, reporting line, and decision authority are defined in Schedule A.

## 3. IP Assignment
All IP created by founders in connection with the Company is assigned to the Company.

## 4. Non-Compete
During employment and for 12 months thereafter, founders shall not engage in competing business activities in the same market segment without written consent.

## 5. Non-Solicitation
Founders may not solicit Company employees or customers for 24 months after departure.

## 6. Confidentiality
Founders maintain strict confidentiality of all Company trade secrets and proprietary information in perpetuity.

## 7. Termination
A founder may be removed from the Company by board vote (75% majority) for cause. Unvested shares are forfeited upon departure.

## 8. Governing Law
Laws of Zimbabwe, jurisdiction:  High Court of Zimbabwe.
