## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Contractor acknowledges that in performing services, Contractor will have access to Confidential Information belonging to Company and its customers.

4.2 Contractor shall: (a) keep Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) use it only to perform services under this Agreement; (c) not disclose it to any third party without written consent; (d) apply at least the same care as Contractor uses to protect their own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.

4.3 "Confidential Information" means source code, product roadmaps, customer data, business strategies, financial information, and any other information designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood as confidential.

4.4 Confidentiality obligations survive termination for 5 years. Trade secrets are protected indefinitely.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 **Assignment.** All work product, inventions, code, designs, documentation, and other creative works produced by Contractor in the performance of services under this Agreement ("Work Product") shall be considered works made for hire. To the extent they do not qualify as works made for hire, Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights.

5.2 **Pre-existing IP.** Contractor retains ownership of any pre-existing tools, frameworks, or code developed independently of this Agreement ("Pre-existing IP"). If Contractor incorporates Pre-existing IP into Work Product, Contractor grants Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual licence to use that Pre-existing IP as part of the Work Product.

5.3 **Assistance.** Contractor shall execute any documents reasonably requested to perfect Company's ownership of Work Product.

5.4 **No Prior IP.** Contractor represents that Work Product will not incorporate any proprietary code or IP from prior employers or clients.

## 6. Non-Solicitation

6.1 During the term and for 12 months after termination, Contractor shall not solicit or attempt to hire any employee or other contractor of Company.

6.2 During the term and for 6 months after termination, Contractor shall not solicit Company's customers whom Contractor directly engaged during the term.

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 If Contractor processes Personal Data (as defined by GDPR or applicable law) on behalf of Company, Contractor shall: (a) process only as instructed by Company; (b) implement appropriate security measures; (c) not subcontract processing without Company's prior written consent; (d) return or destroy Personal Data upon termination.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the SOW is completed, unless terminated earlier.

8.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate on 14 days' written notice.

8.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate immediately for material breach, if uncured within 7 days of written notice.

8.4 **Effect of Termination.** On termination: (a) Company pays for work completed to the termination date; (b) Contractor delivers all Work Product in its current state; (c) each party returns or destroys the other's Confidential Information.
