# Shareholder Agreement — INMARTECH PVT LTD

**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026

## 1. Share Classes
- **Class A (Ordinary):** Founders — 1 vote per share, no dividend preference
- **Class B (Preference):** Investors — 1x liquidation preference, anti-dilution protection

## 2. Transfer Restrictions
Shares may not be transferred without Board approval and right-of-first-refusal to existing shareholders (30-day exercise window).

## 3. Drag-Along
If 75%+ of shareholders approve a sale, remaining shareholders must participate on the same terms.

## 4. Tag-Along
Any shareholder selling 25%+ must allow others to participate pro-rata.

## 5. Board Composition
- 3 seats: 2 Founder-appointed, 1 Investor-appointed
- Board quorum: 2 of 3

## 6. Reserved Matters
The following require 75% shareholder approval: new share issuances, M&A, IP licensing, debt >$500K.

## 7. Governing Law
Laws of Zimbabwe, jurisdiction: Harare High Court.
